NUGSHIP

Sink ships. Mine ORE.

A pixel-art pirate Battleship game for the Solana Seeker. Hide your fleet on a 5×5 sea, lock in the squares you want to fire at, and send your volley. Every volley is a real ORE v3 mining deploy on Solana mainnet, made with your own SOL. When the round ends, the ORE HIT shows the winning square. Nuggy, the ship's cat, sails with you.

How your SOL works

  • One deposit: approve one deposit from your wallet, any amount from 0.01 SOL. It goes into your own ORE account, run by the ORE program and tied to your wallet.
  • No pop-ups in battle: volleys are paid from your deposit. You pick 0.001, 0.005 or 0.01 SOL per square.
  • Fee: 0.00002 SOL per volley. Normal Solana network fees also apply.
  • Your SOL stays yours: NUGSHIP can only fire the squares you lock. Withdraw anytime in Treasure. NUGSHIP never holds your SOL, tokens or keys.

Features

Solo campaign against 5 pirate captains · ORE HIT after every round · Treasure: claim, stake and withdraw · Captain profile and lifetime stats · PvP 1v1 coming soon

Play smart: this game uses real SOL. ORE rounds are random; most volleys lose some or all of the SOL put on them, and winnings are never guaranteed.
