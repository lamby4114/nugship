The game

NUGSHIP is a pirate Battleship game where every cannon volley is a live ORE mining deploy on Solana mainnet using your own SOL. PvP is coming soon.

Live ORE mining and real SOL

  • Deposit: you approve one deposit from your own wallet, any amount from 0.01 SOL, into your own ORE automation account. That account is owned by the ORE program and tied to your wallet. You don't sign each volley; there are no wallet pop-ups during a match.
  • Volleys: NUGSHIP can only fire the squares you lock, at the SOL per square you pick (0.001, 0.005 or 0.01 SOL per square). It can't withdraw your deposit or claim your rewards.
  • Fee: a flat 0.00002 SOL per volley, taken from your deposit. There are no other NUGSHIP fees. Normal Solana network fees apply.
  • Withdraw anytime: from Treasure in the app. NUGSHIP is non-custodial and never holds your SOL, tokens or keys.
  • Risk: ORE rounds are random. Most deploys lose some or all of the SOL used and winnings are never guaranteed. Only play with SOL you can afford to lose. Solana transactions are final; there are no refunds.
