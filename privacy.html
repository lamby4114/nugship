Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what the NUGSHIP app ("we") collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Captain profile: the name you choose and the avatar you pick or upload.
  • Guest ID: a random identifier created on your device to keep your profile and stats.
  • Wallet address: your public Solana address, only if you pair a wallet.
  • Gameplay data: match results, rank and leaderboard stats.
  • Server logs: IP address and connection times, kept for security and abuse prevention.

We never collect your private keys or seed phrase, and we don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

How it's used

To run matchmaking, leaderboards and your profile, to moderate player names and avatars, and to keep the service secure.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: go to Settings › Delete Profile in the app. This removes your profile, avatar and stats from our servers.
  • Report and block: you can report or block other players from their profile card.
  • For any other request, email us at the address below.

Age

NUGSHIP is intended for players aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from minors.

Third parties

The app reads public Solana blockchain data. If you pair a wallet, that wallet app's own privacy policy applies to it.
