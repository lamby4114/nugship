Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy explains what the NUGSHIP app ("we") collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Captain profile: the name you choose and the avatar you pick or upload.
  • Guest ID: a random identifier created on your device to keep your profile and stats.
  • Wallet address: your public Solana address, when you connect a wallet to play live ORE mining.
  • On-chain activity: the ORE automation account, deposits, volleys (deploys), claims and withdrawals tied to your wallet. This data is public on the Solana blockchain; we read it to run matches and show your Treasure and stats.
  • Gameplay data: match results, rank and leaderboard stats.
  • Server logs: IP address and connection times, kept for security and abuse prevention.

NUGSHIP is non-custodial. We never collect or hold your private keys, seed phrase, SOL or tokens, and we don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

How it's used

To run matchmaking, leaderboards and your profile, to moderate player names and avatars, and to keep the service secure.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: open your Captain (profile) screen in the app and tap DELETE PROFILE. This removes your profile, avatar and stats from our servers.
  • Report and block: you can report or block other players from their profile card.
  • For any other request, email us at the address below.

Age

NUGSHIP is intended for players aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from minors.

Third parties

The app reads and writes public Solana blockchain data through the ORE program and Solana RPC providers. Your wallet app (for example via Mobile Wallet Adapter / Seed Vault) has its own privacy policy. Blockchain records are public and permanent and can't be deleted by us.
