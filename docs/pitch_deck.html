THE PROBLEM
02

Mining is passive.

ORE IS ALREADY A GAME... THAT NOBODY PLAYS TOGETHER

  • ORE v3 runs a round about every minute: pick squares on a 5×5 grid, deploy SOL, one square wins.
  • Today you deploy, then you wait. No opponent, no story, no reason to watch the round land.
  • Mobile crypto apps chase “engagement” with points and quests; very few give you a rival.
  • On a phone, a popup per transaction kills any real-time game loop.

WHAT WE MEASURED (READ-ONLY, 14 MAINNET ROUNDS, OCT 7 2026)

Round start-to-start: 289 slots (~77 s) · ~144–152 miners per round · ~10.3–10.8 SOL deployed per round, spread almost evenly over 25 squares.

THE SOLUTION
03

ORE rounds become a naval duel.

THE 5×5 ORE GRID IS THE BATTLEFIELD

  • Squares A1–E5 map 1:1 to ORE’s 25 squares.
  • Hide a fleet: galleon (3), sloop (2), raft (1). It’s sealed as sha256(layout ‖ salt).
  • Salvo = ships still afloat (3→1), +1 bonus shot after a sink or a crit.
  • Both captains aim, then lock on the same slot. Each shot is an ORE deploy on that square.
  • The round settles: hits vs hidden fleets, then ORE’s winning square.
  • A splash can still land on the winning square and mine ORE.

ONE TURN = ONE ORE ROUND
04

~1 minute, four beats.

1 · AIM

Round opens (240-slot deploy window, ~64 s). Tap enemy tiles; the countdown runs on slots, not wall clock.

2 · LOCK

Both players’ targets lock at end_slot − 20 (auto-fire). No peeking: the server only says how many squares the rival picked.

3 · FIRE

Session key sends Checkpoint(prev) + Deploy(mask) in one tx before end_slot. One approval per match.

4 · REVEAL

~48-slot intermission (~13 s), ORE resets, winning square = slot-hash mod 25. Your waters, then theirs.

ORE INTEGRATION
06

ORE is the game clock.

  • Every turn is a live ORE v3 round (program oreV3EG1…ULvWv).
  • Every shot is a real Deploy on that square, the same amount per square, bundled with Checkpoint of the prior round.
  • Crit = hit on ORE’s winning square. Kraken = Motherlode.
  • ORE mined, crits and Kraken sightings sit on the leaderboards next to wins.
  • ORE is the only on-chain product in the game. There is no in-game token, NFT or tradeable item.

WHAT A MATCH CAN DRIVE INTO ORE [ARITHMETIC CEILING, NOT A METRIC]

Up to 4 shots/turn (3 + bonus) × 15-turn cap = ≤60 square-deploys per player per match. At the default 0.005 SOL/shot, that’s ≤0.3 SOL per player per match. Two new ORE miners per duel.

FAIRNESS & TECH
07

Nobody can move their fleet.

COMMIT → PLAY → REVEAL

  • Each phone seals its layout: sha256(layout[25] ‖ salt[32]), with a fresh salt per match.
  • Both commits are exchanged before turn 1.
  • At match end both layouts + salts are revealed and verified on the phone. A mismatch flags the match.
  • Shared test vector checked in both TypeScript and C# tests.

THE REFEREE READS THE CHAIN

A shot only counts if that square has ≥ stake deployed in that round and was locked. The server reads the player’s ORE Miner PDA right after end_slot (DeployEvent logs as fallback). It never signs or sends a transaction.
